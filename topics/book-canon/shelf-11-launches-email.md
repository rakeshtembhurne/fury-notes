# Launches, Webinars, Email/SMS Selling & Self-Publishing — 25-Book Canon
Audience: solopreneur, self-publisher, WhatsApp/email seller. Verified against Open Library (numFound ≥ 1) or major-retailer listings, Sept 2026.

## 1. Launch — Jeff Walker (2014)
Thesis: A sequenced pre-launch (pre-prelaunch, prelaunch, open cart, close) turns attention into event-driven sales.
- Run a 3-video PLC sequence: possibility → transformation → ownership, each ending with engagement homework.
- Seed + trigger list: build a "shot across the bow" list 30 days out, then stack social proof on cart-open day.
- Use scarcity windows (cart closes in 5–7 days) with bonus decay, not discount decay.

## 2. Expert Secrets — Russell Brunson (2017)
Thesis: Experts monetize by building a mass movement around a new opportunity with the Perfect Webinar.
- Deliver the Perfect Webinar arc: origin story → 3 secrets (vehicle, internal, external false beliefs) → stack + close.
- Use Epiphany Bridge stories instead of claims to reframe each false belief before pitching.
- Build the movement with a future-based cause, new opportunity (not improvement offer), and identity for followers.

## 3. DotCom Secrets — Russell Brunson (2015)
Thesis: Funnels plus soap-opera/seinfeld email sequences convert cold traffic into buyers and upsells.
- Structure value ladder: bait → frontend → upsell/downsell → continuity, with one funnel per traffic temperature.
- Run the 5-email Soap Opera Sequence post-opt-in (set the stage, open loops daily, cliffhanger into sale).
- Send daily Seinfeld emails: 90% entertainment + 10% pitch, always with a single CTA.

## 4. Traffic Secrets — Russell Brunson (2020)
Thesis: Fill funnels by fishing where dream customers already congregate (Dream 100) across owned/earned/paid traffic.
- List your Dream 100 (podcasts, newsletters, influencers) and buy/earn presence inside their distribution.
- Publish daily in one primary channel and repurpose everywhere; hook-story-offer in every asset.
- Segment launches by traffic temperature: work affiliates + buyers first, cold traffic only to evergreen.

## 5. Ask — Ryan Levesque (2015)
Thesis: Ask segmented quiz funnels ("buckets") to diagnose buyers, then prescribe the right offer and email track.
- Open with a "deep dive" survey of buyers to mine language, then build a 2–4 bucket quiz landing page.
- Tag quiz outcomes in the ESP and send bucket-specific launch sequences (different stories, same cart).
- Use "micro-commitment" quiz flow: easy question first, ask email after investment, pitch at result page.

## 6. Choose — Ryan Levesque (2019)
Thesis: Pick the right market + business model first (passion markets, evergreen) before building the launch.
- Score ideas on market size, willingness to pay, evergreen demand, and your unfair advantage before committing.
- Validate with a 3-step test: intent search data → Ask survey → paid micro-offer or pre-sale.
- Choose one "orchestra" model (email + launches + evergreen) and ignore shiny-object funnels until $250k+.

## 7. Permission Marketing — Seth Godin (1999)
Thesis: Anticipated, personal, relevant messages (opt-in email/SMS/WhatsApp) beat interruption ads.
- Trade value for consent: lead magnet → explicit opt-in → stated frequency and topic promise.
- Increase permission depth over time: free lesson → quiz → paid trial → VIP list, each step earning more attention.
- Never rent attention you can own: move social followers to email/SMS/WhatsApp within 7 days of contact.

## 8. Email Persuasion — Ian Brodie (2013)
Thesis: Consultants and creators sell via authority-building emails mapped to the buyer journey (know → like → trust → buy).
- Run the P-A-S + proof template: pain, agitate lightly, solve, then one client story and one CTA.
- Build an "autoresponder runway": 7–10 value emails before any pitch to new subscribers.
- End every broadcast with a soft CTA (reply, book, buy) so engagement compounds into deliverability + sales.

## 9. Email Marketing Rules — Chad S. White (2017; 3rd ed.)
Thesis: Deliverability, design, and lifecycle rules determine whether email/SMS sequences reach inboxes and convert.
- Authenticate (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), prune inactives with re-engagement series, and keep list hygiene quarterly.
- Design mobile-first single-column emails: 14px+ body, one CTA button above the fold, plain-text fallback.
- Build lifecycle programs: welcome, browse/cart abandon, post-purchase, win-back — measure per-program revenue.

## 10. Email Marketing Demystified — Matthew Paulson (2015)
Thesis: A blogger's system for scaling to 6-figure lists: math-first opt-ins, automation, and monetization per subscriber.
- Track value per subscriber per month; kill or fix any lead source below your floor.
- Run content upgrades on every post (checklist/template) instead of one generic site-wide magnet.
- Stack monetization: affiliate bridge → tripwire → core offer email, all inside evergreen autoresponders.

## 11. The Ultimate Sales Letter — Dan S. Kennedy (1990; 3rd ed. 2011)
Thesis: A fixed 28-step sales-letter skeleton (headline → offer → urgency) sells in any medium, including email and webinar scripts.
- Open with headline + deck promising a specific outcome for a specific reader, never cleverness.
- Build the offer stack: core + premiums + guarantee + reason-why discount + deadline.
- Use Johnson boxes, bullets of benefits, and P.S. restating offer + urgency in long emails and launch pages.

## 12. No B.S. Direct Marketing — Dan S. Kennedy with Marty Fort (2006 ed.)
Thesis: Direct-response discipline (track everything, mail often, sell hard) applies to email, SMS, and launches.
- Mail/email the buyer list relentlessly: monthly minimum, weekly for hot segments — "the money is in the list you mail."
- Score every campaign on cost per acquisition and lifetime value, not opens or likes.
- Layer offline-online: postcard/SMS/WhatsApp reminders around every webinar and cart-close deadline.

## 13. The Copywriter's Handbook — Robert W. Bly (3rd ed. 2006)
Thesis: Tested headline, lead, and body formulas make emails, ads, and landing pages measurably convert.
- Write 10+ headlines per email/landing page; lead with news, benefit, or curiosity gap, then test.
- Use the AIDA + proof flow: attention headline, interest lead, desire bullets/testimonials, action close with deadline.
- Keep B2B/creator emails short-paragraph, skimmable, one idea per email, one CTA per email.

## 14. Tested Advertising Methods — John Caples (1932; 4th ed. 1998)
Thesis: Only tested appeals sell — headlines and offers proven in small tests scale to big launches.
- Test headlines first (they carry ~70% of response): benefit + specificity + curiosity beats brand.
- Test one variable at a time on small email segments before rolling out to the full list.
- Steal winners: swipe proven headline/offer templates (how-to, numbers, warning) and adapt to your niche.

## 15. Everybody Writes — Ann Handley (2014)
Thesis: Non-writers win email/content by writing useful, specific, human prose with ruthless editing.
- Apply the 12-step publishing checklist: goal, voice, ugly first draft, second-pair eyes, one CTA.
- Write to one person in plain words; cut jargon, show don't tell, use concrete numbers and stories.
- Repurpose one strong idea into email → WhatsApp broadcast → landing FAQ → webinar slide.

## 16. Copywriting Secrets — Jim Edwards (2020)
Thesis: Short-form hooks, bullets, and closes plug into any email, SMS, or webinar slide to lift conversions.
- Open loops in subject lines and first lines; close them after the click or inside the lesson.
- Stack "fascinations" (benefit bullets) before revealing price on sales pages and webinar stacks.
- Use assumptive closes and 3-way tie-downs ("deadline / bonus / guarantee") in cart-close emails.

## 17. Teach and Grow Rich — Danny Iny (2015)
Thesis: Sell courses from audience + pilot first: teach free, pre-sell a pilot cohort, then build the full course.
- Run a free "seed" workshop, collect objections, then pre-sell the paid pilot before recording anything.
- Price pilots low with feedback obligations; raise price each cohort as testimonials accrue.
- Teach one transformation per module with worksheets; completion (not content volume) drives refunds down.

## 18. Will It Fly? — Pat Flynn (2016)
Thesis: Validate book/course/launch ideas with lean tests before investing months of production.
- Run the airport test: idea → market history → 1-on-1 conversations → small experiment (landing + pre-order).
- Force a purchase signal (pre-sale, deposit, waitlist with payment) before building the product.
- Niche by "super-specific + transformation": own a small pond (e.g., sourdough for diabetics) first.

## 19. Superfans — Pat Flynn (2019)
Thesis: Launches succeed on superfans built with tiny personal moments, not mass traffic.
- Engineer "just-for-you" moments: reply videos, name shoutouts, fast DMs to new subscribers.
- Build community rituals (weekly wins thread, challenge cohorts) that turn buyers into evangelists.
- Let superfans co-create: testimonials, affiliate army, and launch-day social proof squads.

## 20. $100M Offers — Alex Hormozi (2021)
Thesis: Make offers so good they feel stupid to refuse: dream outcome × likelihood ÷ time ÷ effort, with risk reversal.
- Name, price, and stack value: list every component with dollar values to widen the value-price gap.
- Add scarcity (limited seats/bonuses), urgency (cohort/cart close), and a bold conditional guarantee.
- Use the offer in webinars/challenges: promise → mechanism → stack → guarantee → deadline CTA.

## 21. Your First 1000 Copies — Tim Grahl (2013)
Thesis: Authors sell books by building connections (permission + content + outreach) before and after launch day.
- Build in phases: platform (email list) → content + influencer outreach → launch team → evergreen.
- Recruit 50–200 launch-team readers with ARC copies for week-one reviews and shares.
- Run outreach sprints: 10 influencers/day, personalized, offering value (guest post, audience) before asking.

## 22. APE: Author, Publisher, Entrepreneur — Guy Kawasaki & Shawn Welch (2013)
Thesis: Self-publishers win by owning publishing (tools + quality) and acting as entrepreneurs (marketing engine).
- Publish with pro standards: developmental edit, copyedit, pro cover, print + ebook + POD from day one.
- Price/promote with data: pre-orders, KDP Select windows, and cross-promo with adjacent authors.
- Treat the book as a business card for courses/consulting: CTA inside front + back matter to email list.

## 23. Write. Publish. Repeat. — Sean Platt & Johnny B. Truant (2014)
Thesis: Fiction/nonfiction income compounds from rapid production + series thinking + direct audience ownership.
- Publish in series/clusters so each book sells the others (read-through funnels + box sets).
- Ship on cadence: outline → dictation/draft sprints → rapid editing pipeline, monthly or quarterly.
- Own the list: back-matter magnet + welcome sequence per series, then launch each book to buyers first.

## 24. How to Market a Book — Joanna Penn (2014; updated eds.)
Thesis: Discoverability + direct sales for indie authors: optimize retailers, then drive owned-traffic launches.
- Optimize metadata: keyword-rich titles/subtitles, 7 KDP keywords, categories, and A+ content.
- Build launch assets: author site + email magnet + ARC team + editorial calendar for the 90 days around release.
- Diversify: wide vs. exclusive, audio/print/ebook, plus direct sales (Gumroad/Shopify) for higher margins.

## 25. Mobile Marketing — Daniel Rowles (2013)
Thesis: Mobile (SMS/WhatsApp/app/email-on-phone) wins when messages are short, timely, location-aware, and opt-in.
- Use SMS/WhatsApp for time-critical launch triggers: webinar starting, cart closing, bonus expiring — short + link.
- Keep mobile emails under 50-character subjects and single-thumb CTAs; test on devices, not desktops.
- Get explicit mobile opt-ins with clear value/frequency disclosure; honor quiet hours and instant opt-out.
