# Community, Memberships, Affiliates & Partnerships — 25-Book Canon

> All 25 verified against Open Library (openlibrary.org/search.json). Note: Kevin Kelly's "1,000 True Fans" (2008) is an essay, not a book, so it is excluded; its thesis (a creator can make a living with 1,000 fans paying $100/yr via direct channels) is covered in practice by Superfans (#10) and Membership Economy (#7).

## 1. Tribes — Seth Godin (2008)
**Thesis:** The internet lets anyone lead a tribe of people connected by shared belief — leadership, not authority, is the scarce asset.
- **Tactics:** (1) Name the shared belief in one sentence and repeat it everywhere. (2) Give members a way to talk to each other, not just to you. (3) Lead by going first — ship the manifesto, event, or ritual yourself.
- Verified: OL `Tribes / Seth Godin / 2008`.

## 2. The Art of Community — Jono Bacon (2009; 2nd ed 2012)
**Thesis:** Communities are built systems: strategy, governance, and contribution pipelines beat vibes.
- **Tactics:** (1) Write a one-page community strategy (mission, audience, success metrics). (2) Build contribution ladders: reader → contributor → maintainer. (3) Measure belonging with activity + sentiment, not just headcount.
- Verified: OL `The Art of Community / Jono Bacon / 2009`.

## 3. Get Together — Bailey Richardson, Kevin Huynh, Kai Elmer Sotto (2019)
**Thesis:** Great communities are grown through small, repeatable gatherings with a clear purpose, not big launches.
- **Tactics:** (1) Start with 5–15 people and a sharp "who it's for" filter. (2) Give every gathering a job to do (make, decide, celebrate). (3) Spotlight members' wins publicly to fuel identity.
- Verified: OL `Get Together / Richardson, Huynh, Sotto / 2019`.

## 4. People Powered — Jono Bacon (2019)
**Thesis:** Community participation is a growth engine when you design value exchange for every member segment.
- **Tactics:** (1) Map member personas to what each gets and gives. (2) Run onboarding sprints that get a newcomer to first contribution in 7 days. (3) Tie community KPIs to business KPIs (activation, retention, expansion).
- Verified: OL `People Powered / Jono Bacon / 2019`.

## 5. Community Building on the Web — Amy Jo Kim (2000)
**Thesis:** Durable online communities run on membership lifecycles and explicit social roles, a playbook unchanged since the early web.
- **Tactics:** (1) Design the visitor → novice → regular → leader → elder journey. (2) Codify roles with powers and visible status. (3) Seed purpose-built gathering places (events, rituals) before scaling.
- Verified: OL `Community building on the Web / Amy Jo Kim / 2000`.

## 6. Building Brand Communities — Carrie Melissa Jones & Charles Vogl (2020)
**Thesis:** Brand communities compound loyalty when the brand hosts, but members own the meaning.
- **Tactics:** (1) Pick one community archetype (support, advocacy, product, belonging) and staff for it. (2) Co-create a code of conduct with founding members. (3) Report community-sourced product insights to the roadmap quarterly.
- Verified: OL `Building Brand Communities / Jones, Vogl / 2020`.

## 7. The Membership Economy — Robbie Kellman Baxter (2015)
**Thesis:** Customers pay for ongoing belonging and outcomes (membership), not one-time ownership.
- **Tactics:** (1) Reframe pricing around forever-value: onboarding → habit → anniversary. (2) Build a "membership mindset" dashboard (churn, expansion, referral). (3) Create insider benefits that cost little but signal status.
- Verified: OL `The membership economy / Robbie Kellman Baxter / 2015`.

## 8. The Forever Transaction — Robbie Kellman Baxter (2020)
**Thesis:** Retention is a product: engineer the first 90 days and every renewal moment or subscriptions leak.
- **Tactics:** (1) Script the first-use, first-win, and first-anniversary moments. (2) Segment at-risk members by behavior, then trigger human outreach. (3) Price tiers against outcomes achieved, not features gated.
- Verified: OL `The Forever Transaction / Robbie Kellman Baxter / 2020`.

## 9. Subscribed — Tien Tzuo with Gabe Weisert (2018)
**Thesis:** Subscriptions flip the company from shipping products to growing recurring relationships (the "subscribed economy").
- **Tactics:** (1) Instrument MRR/ARR, net retention, and time-to-value as exec metrics. (2) Sell outcomes with land-and-expand pods, not big-bang deals. (3) Rebuild comp around retention, not just new logos.
- Verified: OL `Subscribed / Tien Tzuo / 2018`.

## 10. Superfans — Pat Flynn (2019)
**Thesis:** A small core of superfans drives disproportionate growth if you systematically turn casual followers into insiders.
- **Tactics:** (1) Run the "pyramid of fandom": casual → active → connected → superfan touchpoints. (2) Create insider rituals (early access, secret drops). (3) Feature fan stories as the main social proof asset.
- Verified: OL `Superfans / Pat Flynn / 2019`.

## 11. Fanocracy — David Meerman Scott & Reiko Scott (2020)
**Thesis:** Fandom is neuroscience: proximity and shared identity convert customers into passionate advocates.
- **Tactics:** (1) Engineer proximity — founders visible, accessible, human. (2) Let fans co-create (names, designs, lore). (3) Reward the top 1% with access, not discounts.
- Verified: OL `Fanocracy / Reiko Scott et al. / 2020`.

## 12. Hug Your Haters — Jay Baer (2016)
**Thesis:** Complaints in public channels are spectacles — answering every one is the cheapest marketing there is.
- **Tactics:** (1) Answer 100% of complaints on every channel, fast. (2) Split onstage (public reply) vs offstage (resolution) handling. (3) Mine hate for the top 3 fixable frictions each quarter.
- Verified: OL `Hug your haters / Jay Baer / 2016`.

## 13. Talk Triggers — Jay Baer & Daniel Lemin (2018)
**Thesis:** Word of mouth is engineered: build one remarkable, repeatable operational differentiator customers must retell.
- **Tactics:** (1) Design a talk trigger that is remarkable, relevant, reasonable, repeatable. (2) Operationalize it into SOPs so every customer experiences it. (3) Seed the story with share-ready prompts at the moment of delight.
- Verified: OL `Talk triggers / Jay Baer / 2018`.

## 14. The Referral Engine — John Jantsch (2010)
**Thesis:** Referrals are a system, not luck: teach partners who to send and make sending effortless.
- **Tactics:** (1) Write your ideal-referral one-pager for partners. (2) Build a referral kit: intro script, one-slide proof, booking link. (3) Run a monthly partner touch (win-share, ask, reciprocate).
- Verified: OL `The Referral Engine / John Jantsch / 2010`.

## 15. Affiliate Program Management: An Hour a Day — Evgenii Prussakov (2011)
**Thesis:** Affiliate programs compound when managed as a portfolio: recruit, activate, optimize, prune — daily.
- **Tactics:** (1) Timebox daily ops: recruit 30 min, activate 20 min, optimize 10 min. (2) Tier affiliates (VIP/growth/long-tail) with different commissions. (3) Kill fraud and coupon-poaching with attribution rules.
- Verified: OL `Affiliate program management / Evgenii Prussakov / 2011`.

## 16. A Practical Guide to Affiliate Marketing — Evgenii Prussakov (2007)
**Thesis:** Affiliate success = right merchants × right publishers × clean tracking; fundamentals beat hacks.
- **Tactics:** (1) Vet programs on cookie window, payout reliability, EPC. (2) Match offer intent to content intent before promoting. (3) Disclose and pre-sell honestly to protect list trust.
- Verified: OL `A Practical Guide to Affiliate Marketing / Evgenii Prussakov / 2007`.

## 17. The Community Manager's Playbook — Lauren Perkins (2014)
**Thesis:** Community management is an operating role: listen, engage, measure, and convert social capital into revenue.
- **Tactics:** (1) Stand up a listening dashboard across owned + earned channels. (2) Run a 90-day content/engagement calendar tied to launches. (3) Report monthly: growth, engagement, and pipeline influence.
- Verified: OL `The Community Manager's Playbook / Lauren Perkins / 2014`.

## 18. Tribal Leadership — Dave Logan, John King & Halee Fischer-Wright (2008)
**Thesis:** Organizations perform at the level of their culture's "tribal stage" (1–5); leaders upgrade culture one triad at a time.
- **Tactics:** (1) Diagnose your team's stage by listening to language ("my life sucks" → "life is great"). (2) Form triads around shared values and noble causes. (3) Coach Stage-4 behaviors: peer accountability, shared wins.
- Verified: OL `Tribal leadership / Logan et al. / 2008`.

## 19. The Starfish and the Spider — Ori Brafman & Rod Beckstrom (2006)
**Thesis:** Decentralized (starfish) organizations outlast centralized ones because there is no head to cut off.
- **Tactics:** (1) Push power to circles/chapters with local autonomy. (2) Replace HQ control with shared ideology + trust norms. (3) Use catalysts (connectors), not CEOs, to spark local action.
- Verified: OL `The Starfish and the Spider / Brafman, Beckstrom / 2006–2008`.

## 20. The Art of Gathering — Priya Parker (2018)
**Thesis:** Every gathering needs a disputable purpose; design for that purpose and exclude everything else.
- **Tactics:** (1) Write a purpose statement that rules things out. (2) Prime guests pre-event and close decisively. (3) Engineer the room (seating, entry, first 5 minutes) for connection.
- Verified: OL `The Art of Gathering / Priya Parker / 2018`.

## 21. The Culture Code — Daniel Coyle (2018)
**Thesis:** Great cultures are built from three skills: safety, vulnerability, and purpose — all learnable behaviors.
- **Tactics:** (1) Over-signal belonging cues in onboarding. (2) Leaders go vulnerable first to unlock candor. (3) Translate purpose into visible daily behaviors, not slogans.
- Verified: OL `The culture code / Daniel Coyle / 2018`.

## 22. Here Comes Everybody — Clay Shirky (2008)
**Thesis:** Near-zero organizing costs let loose groups do what once required institutions — design for self-assembly.
- **Tactics:** (1) Lower the cost of joining and contributing to near zero. (2) Provide lightweight coordination tools, not hierarchies. (3) Expect and design for failure modes (spam, splits) at scale.
- Verified: OL `Here comes everybody / Clay Shirky / 2008`.

## 23. Cult Status — Tim Duggan (2020)
**Thesis:** Loyal "cult" brands are built by standing for something polarizing and rewarding insiders relentlessly.
- **Tactics:** (1) Declare an enemy or a bold stance worth joining. (2) Build rituals and language only insiders get. (3) Monetize belonging: members-first drops, events, tiers.
- Verified: OL `Cult Status / Tim Duggan / 2020`.

## 24. Never Lose a Customer Again — Joey Coleman (2018)
**Thesis:** The first 100 days decide loyalty; engineer all eight phases of the customer journey or churn is baked in.
- **Tactics:** (1) Map the 8 phases (assess → admit → affirm → activate → acclimate → accomplish → adopt → advocate). (2) Script remarkable touches in each phase. (3) Assign journey owners with retention bonuses.
- Verified: OL `Never Lose a Customer Again / Joey Coleman / 2018`.

## 25. The Power of Moments — Chip Heath & Dan Heath (2017)
**Thesis:** Loyalty is built from defining moments — elevation, insight, pride, connection — which can be designed.
- **Tactics:** (1) Audit the journey for pits, then fill with peaks. (2) Script milestone moments (first win, anniversary, graduation). (3) Multiply connection moments: shared struggle + shared meaning.
- Verified: OL `The Power of Moments / Chip & Dan Heath / 2017`.
